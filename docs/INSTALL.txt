Mac Pulse 1.3.0

Requirements
Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later), macOS 14.2 or later.

Install
1. Drag Mac Pulse.app to the Applications folder beside it.
2. Eject this disk image.
3. Open Mac Pulse from Applications.

Previously called Mac Monitor. Quit Mac Monitor before opening Mac Pulse.
Your existing history, settings and Cleanup state are retained.

This community build is signed locally (ad hoc), rather than with a Developer
ID certificate, and is not notarized by Apple. If macOS blocks the first launch,
review the source and download, then use System Settings > Privacy & Security >
Open Anyway to approve this specific app. Apple's instructions:
https://support.apple.com/102445

The dashboard and menu-bar panel show live readings. Click a card to open its
tab. Clicking outside the menu-bar panel dismisses it. Closing the dashboard
keeps the monitor running; use Quit or Command-Q to exit completely.

Optional access
Per-app audio mixing requires macOS system-audio permission. You can enable it
under Privacy & Security > Screen & System Audio Recording and relaunch.
Bluetooth device discovery starts when you click Read Bluetooth Devices.
Notifications and launch at login are enabled from the app's Settings.

Cleanup
Open Cleanup or press Command-Shift-K for the integrated BlitzClean tools.
Choose Review cleanup to scan caches, developer files, Docker, simulators and
worktrees. Review and confirm selected removals. Personal-file removal uses
Trash; eligible caches and developer cleanup are removed permanently.
Cleanup > Setup offers optional Full Disk Access and Accessibility settings.
Media export needs an existing ffmpeg/ffprobe installation and keeps originals.
Cleanup history is stored under:
~/Library/Application Support/MacMonitor/Cleanup

Your data stays on this Mac. History is stored in:
~/Library/Application Support/MacMonitor/history.sqlite

Hardware readings vary by Mac. Dashes mean a reading is unavailable. Desktop
Macs do not have an internal battery. Fan speeds are read-only; manual fan
control is not included. This app does not require an account or a web server.
